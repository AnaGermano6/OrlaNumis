## Collection

- **[Collection Management](collection-management.md)** — Browse, filter, group, search and organize coins and banknotes
- **[Coin Details](coin-details.md)** — Numismatic data, images, catalog references, market value, sets, journal and timeline
- **[Bulk Operations](bulk-operations.md)** — Select several coins to add them to a set or delete them

## Discovery & Acquisition

- **[Quick Capture](../quick-capture.md)** — Mobile-first intake drafts for show-floor photos and notes, promoted later to the collection or wish list
- **[Coin Lookup](coin-lookup.md)** — Photograph a coin or slab, extract NGC certs, verify with NGC, and save to the wish list or collection
- **[Wish List](wish-list.md)** — Track coins to acquire, with availability checks, saved search alerts and AI search
- **[Numista Catalog Lookup](numista-integration.md)** — Find Numista types, prefill coins and save structured catalog references

## AI Features

All AI features are optional and need an AI provider configured by an administrator.

- **[AI Coin Analysis](ai-analysis.md)** — Vision-model analysis of obverse/reverse photos using Anthropic Claude or Ollama
- **[Deep Analysis](deep-analysis.md)** — Resumable identification with Numista evidence and confirm-gated proposals
- **[Coin Agent](ai-search-agent.md)** — Chat with an AI agent to find coins, answer collection questions and research shows
- **[Coin Copilot](coin-copilot.md)** — Default-off, read-only multi-step analysis over your own collection data
- **[How Coin Copilot Works](how-coin-copilot-works.md)** — Every helper, where it looks, and what it will never do
- **[AI Grading Assistant](ai-grading.md)** — Estimate grades from photos with reasoning and confidence
- **[Price Trend Analysis](price-trends.md)** — Market trend analysis for a coin or type
- **[Collection Gap Analysis](gap-analysis.md)** — Suggestions for coins missing from the collection
- **[Coin Photography Guide](photography-guide.md)** — Critiques and tips for better coin photos
- **[Similar Lot Finder](similar-lots.md)** — Find auction listings similar to a coin
